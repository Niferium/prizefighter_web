Fighting Styles We Teach

Boxing

The sweet science - master footwork, head movement, and devastating punch combinations.

  • Technical striking skills
  • Defensive mastery
  • Conditioning & stamina
  • Competition preparation

Muay Thai

The art of eight limbs - using fists, elbows, knees, and shins for devastating combat.

  • Eight-point striking system
  • Clinch techniques
  • Powerful leg kicks
  • Traditional Thai training

Kickboxing

Dynamic striking combining punches and kicks for a complete stand-up fighting system.

  • Explosive combinations
  • High & low kicks
  • Cardio intensive
  • Full-body workout

Mixed Martial Arts

Complete fighting system integrating striking, grappling, and ground fighting techniques.

  • Stand-up striking
  • Takedowns & wrestling
  • Ground & pound
  • Submission grappling

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu

Ground fighting art focusing on leverage, technique, and submissions over strength.

  • Positional control
  • Joint locks & chokes
  • Guard techniques
  • Self-defense focus

Wrestling

Elite grappling discipline emphasizing takedowns, control, and physical dominance.

  • Explosive takedowns
  • Superior positioning
  • Physical conditioning
  • Mental toughness
